Questions.

How is this different from Screen Time? +
Screen Time lives on the phone you're trying to escape. One tap dismisses it. Tyme Boxed lives outside your phone. To unlock, you walk. There is no Ignore button.
What if I have an emergency? +
Calls, messages, and maps always stay on. The app also has a few emergency unlocks that work without the device. You're never stranded.
Can I just delete the app to bypass? +
No. Strict Mode doesn't let you uninstall the app.
Does it need charging or Wi-Fi? +
No. No battery, no Wi-Fi, no charging. Your phone's tap powers it.
What if I lose it? +
Replacements at a discount for existing customers.
What's included in ₹999? +
The device, the iPhone, Android and Mac apps, and every future update. One payment. No subscriptions, ever.
Android or Mac? +
Yes to both. The Android app is live on Google Play, and the Mac app is on the Mac App Store. On iPhone and Android the app blocks distracting apps. The Mac app blocks distracting websites and follows your iPhone: tap your Tyme Boxed on your phone, and your Mac picks up the same session through iCloud.
